How to Build a Resume + Check ATS Score

Build a professional resume with a real filled example, then check your ATS match against any job description — all free, no signup.

Step 1
Choose a template
Open ResumeKit. Pick a template that matches the job:
  • Classic — Clean and traditional. Works for all industries. Best for formal roles (finance, government, law).
  • Modern — Left-sidebar with colour accent. Great for tech, design, and product roles.
  • Compact — Dense format fitting more content on one page. Good if you have 5+ years of experience.
Step 2
Fill your contact information

Step 3
Write a professional summary
2–3 sentences at the top. This is the first thing a recruiter reads. Mention your years of experience, key skills, and what you're looking for.
Example summary
Full-stack developer with 4 years of experience building scalable web applications using React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL. Delivered 3 production SaaS products serving 10,000+ monthly users. Seeking a senior engineering role where I can contribute to product architecture and mentor junior developers.
Step 4

List from most recent to oldest. Use action verbs and include measurable outcomes wherever possible.

Click the ATS ✓ tab at the top. Paste the job description you want to apply to in the text area. Click Check ATS Score.

Step 8
Download as PDF
Click "⬇️ Download PDF" at the top-right of the preview. In the browser print dialog:
  • Destination: Save as PDF
  • Paper size: A4
  • Margins: Default
  • Uncheck "Headers and footers"
💡 Save a separate PDF for each company you apply to (e.g. Resume_Flipkart.pdf, Resume_Google.pdf). Tailored resumes get 2–3× more callbacks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS and how does it work?

ATS (Applicant Tracking System) is software used by companies to filter resumes automatically. It extracts keywords from the job description and checks if your resume contains them. Resumes that don't match enough keywords are filtered out before any human reads them. Our ATS checker replicates this process client-side so you can optimise your resume before submitting.

Is my resume stored on ResumeKit's servers?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your data is never sent to any server. When you close the tab, the data is cleared. For this reason, copy your text to a document elsewhere if you want to save it between sessions.

Can I write a cover letter here too?

Yes — ResumeKit includes a Cover Letter builder. The nav bar at the top has a ✉️ Cover Letter link. It follows the same workflow: fill the form, pick a template, download as PDF.

Should I include a photo in my resume?

In India, including a photo is common and often expected. In the US, UK, or Europe, photos are generally not included to avoid bias. Choose based on the country/company you're applying to.
